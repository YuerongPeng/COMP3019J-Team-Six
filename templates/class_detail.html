Cancellation

  • A booked place can be cancelled until two hours before the start. If the card counts visits, the visit goes back to that same card, and the place is offered to the next person waiting.
  • Inside those two hours the booking stays and the visit is not returned.
  • If staff moved the start into that two-hour window, a member who was already booked can still cancel until the class starts.
  • Once the class has started, it cannot be cancelled.

Waitlist

  • You can join only when the class is full. Waiting does not hold a seat and does not use a visit.
  • A free place is offered to position 1, the person who has waited longest. They have 30 minutes to confirm. The class is not booked until they confirm.
  • If they decline, or the 30 minutes pass, the place goes to the next position. Joining again after that puts you at the back.
  • Once the class has started, no further offers are made. When it ends, people still waiting or still unconfirmed are removed, and no visit is charged.
